School Selection

Parents and guardians play an important role in choosing the secondary school their child would like to attend.

Under MOE PEP arrangements, parents are allowed to select seven secondary schools in order of preference.

How school selection works

First Choice: Select the school your child would most like to attend.

Second Choice: Select another preferred school.

Third Choice: Select another suitable option.

Fourth Choice: Add another school.

Fifth Choice: Add another school.

Sixth Choice: Select from the designated school cluster.

Seventh Choice: Select another school from the designated cluster.

Important things parents should consider

  • School location and transportation.
  • The child's interests and academic needs.
  • School programmes and available facilities.
  • Single-sex or co-educational schools.
  • The order in which schools are selected.

How does school placement work?

The Ministry considers students' PEP performance, school preferences and available spaces when assigning secondary school placements.

Please note: Selecting a school does not guarantee placement there.
