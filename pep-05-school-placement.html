School Placement

School placement is the process through which Jamaica's Ministry of Education assigns students to secondary schools after completing their Primary Exit Profile (PEP) assessments.

The placement process uses students' combined assessment results from Grades 4, 5 and 6, together with their selected school preferences and the availability of spaces at receiving schools.

What Happens If a Student Does Not Get Their Preferred School?

If a student cannot be accommodated at any of their selected schools, the Ministry may use alternative placement arrangements.

Proximity Placement: The student may be assigned to a suitable secondary school based on proximity and available spaces.

Manual Placement: The Ministry reviews cases requiring individual placement.

Important Information for Parents

  • Selecting a school does not guarantee placement at that institution.
  • A student's performance and available school spaces influence placement.
  • Parents should follow the receiving school's registration instructions once placement is announced.
  • Transfers after placement are not automatic and are subject to Ministry procedures.
Remember: Placement is based on performance, preferences, and available spaces.
