GRADE 5 - LANGUAGE ARTS

MOE National Standards Curriculum - Grade 5 | PEP Performance Task Prep

✏️ PRACTICE - Grade 5 Language Arts 📝 PERFORMANCE TASKS - Language Arts

1. Reading & Comprehension - MOE Grade 5

What MOE expects: Read fluently, find main idea, details, sequence, cause & effect, make inferences.

  • Main idea & supporting details
  • Sequence, cause & effect, compare & contrast
  • Inference - what is not directly stated
  • Fact vs Opinion
  • Story elements: character, setting, plot, theme

2. Vocabulary & Word Study

What MOE expects: Use context clues, prefixes, suffixes, synonyms, antonyms.

  • Context clues
  • Prefixes: un-, re-, mis-, pre-
  • Suffixes: -ful, -less, -ment, -ness
  • Synonyms, antonyms, homophones
  • Dictionary skills

3. Grammar & Punctuation

What MOE expects: Correct sentence structure, parts of speech, subject-verb agreement.

  • Nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns
  • Subject-verb agreement
  • Past, present & future tenses
  • Capital letters, full stops, commas, question marks, exclamation marks
  • Apostrophes for possession & contractions

4. Writing - MOE Grade 5

What MOE expects: Plan, draft, revise and edit. Write clear paragraphs with topic sentences.

  • Narrative: story with beginning, middle, end
  • Descriptive: describe person, place, event
  • Persuasive / Opinion: give reasons for your view
  • Letter writing: friendly & formal
  • Paragraphs: topic sentence + supporting sentences

5. Listening, Speaking & Spelling

What MOE expects: Listen for information, speak clearly, spell Grade 5 words correctly.

  • Listening for main idea & details
  • Oral presentation - speak in complete sentences
  • Spelling patterns: -tion, -sion, -ough, double letters
  • Jamaican Standard English vs Jamaican Creole - when to use each

6. PEP Performance Task - Language Arts

Grade 5 PEP Language Arts is a Performance Task - not multiple choice. You will:

  • Read a passage
  • Answer 4-6 open-ended questions
  • Explain your answer with evidence from the passage
  • Write a short paragraph / letter

Tip: Always use evidence: "The passage says..." + your own words.

Aligned to Jamaica MOE National Standards Curriculum (NSC) Grade 5 Language Arts. Grade 5 PEP = Performance Tasks in Language Arts & Mathematics only.
