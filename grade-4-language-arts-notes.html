📘 Grade 4 Language Arts Notes

Read first, then watch, then practise

1. Comprehension

Read the passage 2 times. Read the question. Go back and find the answer in the passage. Do not guess from memory.

2. Nouns & Pronouns

Noun: person, place, thing - teacher, Kingston, book
Pronoun: replaces noun - he, she, it, they, we
Example: Mary is kind. She helps everyone.

3. Verbs & Tenses

Verb: action word - run, jump, read
Past: walked | Present: walks | Future: will walk

4. Adjectives & Adverbs

Adjective: describes a noun - big dog, sweet mango
Adverb: describes a verb - runs quickly, speaks softly

5. Punctuation

Full stop . ends a sentence. Question mark ? for questions. Capital letter starts a sentence and for names.

6. Spelling & Vocabulary

Synonym: same meaning - happy = glad
Antonym: opposite - hot = cold

7. Writing

Story = Beginning - Middle - End.
Letter = Date - Greeting - Body - Closing - Name.
Always check spelling and punctuation at the end.
