Education Regions

Jamaica's Ministry of Education divides the island into administrative education regions to help manage and supervise schools and educational services.

These regions allow the Ministry to coordinate educational activities, support schools and provide services to students, teachers and parents.

Jamaica's Education Regions

REGION 2 – Port Antonio

4

REGION 4 – Montego Bay

5

REGION 5 – Mandeville

6

REGION 6 – Old Harbour
Parishes: St. Catherine and Clarendon.

What Do Education Regions Do?

Regional offices help to:

  • Supervise and support schools.
  • Coordinate educational programmes.
  • Assist teachers and school administrators.
  • Monitor school operations and educational standards.
  • Support the delivery of Ministry policies and services.

Find Your Education Region

Parents can identify their child's education region using the parish where the school is located.

Tip: Find your region by checking the parish where your child's school is located.
