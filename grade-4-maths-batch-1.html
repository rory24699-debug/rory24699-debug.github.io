Grade 4 Mathematics – Mock Test 1

Topic: Cabbage

Choose the correct answer for each question.

1. A farmer plants 6 rows of cabbage with 8 plants in each row. How many cabbage plants are there altogether?

2. A farmer harvests 120 cabbages and packs them equally into 10 boxes. How many cabbages are in each box?

3. A cabbage weighs 2 kilograms. What is the total weight of 5 cabbages?

4. A farmer sells 35 cabbages in the morning and 28 in the afternoon. How many cabbages does he sell altogether?

5. A farmer has 90 cabbages. He sells 36. How many cabbages are left?

6. Each cabbage costs $150. How much will 4 cabbages cost?
