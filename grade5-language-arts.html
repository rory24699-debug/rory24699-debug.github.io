Batch 1 - Reading & Comprehension

MOE Grade 5: Main idea, details, sequence, cause & effect, inference, fact vs opinion.

  • What is the main idea of the passage?
  • Find 2 details that support the main idea
  • Is this statement fact or opinion? Explain why

Batch 2 - Vocabulary & Word Study

MOE Grade 5: Context clues, prefixes, suffixes, synonyms, antonyms.

  • Use context clues: What does "brave" mean in this sentence?
  • Add a prefix to "happy" to mean "not happy"
  • Give a synonym for "rapid"

Batch 3 - Grammar & Punctuation

MOE Grade 5: Parts of speech, subject-verb agreement, tenses, punctuation.

  • Identify the verb: "The children play happily."
  • Correct this: "She go to school every day."
  • Add the correct punctuation: "Where are you going"

Batch 4 - Writing

MOE Grade 5: Narrative, descriptive, persuasive, letter writing. Topic sentence + supporting sentences.

  • Write a paragraph with a topic sentence about your best friend
  • Write a persuasive paragraph: Why should school start later?
  • Write a friendly letter to your cousin

Batch 5 - Listening, Speaking & Spelling

MOE Grade 5: Listen for main idea, speak clearly, spell Grade 5 words.

  • Listen: What is the main idea?
  • Spell: "beautiful", "because", "friend"
  • Jamaican Standard English vs Jamaican Creole - when to use each
