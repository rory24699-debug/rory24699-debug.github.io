GRADE 5 – MATHEMATICS

Curriculum Notes & Worked Examples

1. Whole Numbers and Place Value

Whole numbers are numbers without fractions or decimal parts. Each digit has a value based on its position.

Place Value Chart

In the number 483,726:

  • 4 = Hundred Thousands
  • 8 = Ten Thousands
  • 3 = Thousands
  • 7 = Hundreds
  • 2 = Tens
  • 6 = Ones
Worked Example
What is the value of 8 in 483,726?
Answer: 80,000.

2. Addition and Subtraction

Addition combines numbers. Subtraction finds the difference between numbers or how much remains.

Worked Example – Addition

24,568 + 13,247
= 37,815

Worked Example – Subtraction

52,430 − 18,215
= 34,215
Remember: Line up digits according to their place values before calculating.

3. Multiplication

Multiplication is repeated addition. It helps us find the total when equal groups are combined.

Worked Example
125 × 4
= 100 × 4 + 20 × 4 + 5 × 4
= 400 + 80 + 20
= 500

4. Division

Division means sharing a quantity into equal groups or finding how many times one number fits into another.

Worked Example
156 ÷ 4 = 39
Check: 39 × 4 = 156.
Remember: Multiplication can be used to check a division answer.

5. Fractions

A fraction represents part of a whole. The numerator is the top number and the denominator is the bottom number.

Example: In 3/4:
3 is the numerator.
4 is the denominator.
It means 3 out of 4 equal parts.

Equivalent Fractions

Equivalent fractions have the same value.

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6

6. Decimals

Decimals represent parts of a whole using a decimal point.

Example: 4.35
4 = Ones
3 = Tenths
5 = Hundredths

7. Measurement

Measurement helps us describe length, mass, capacity and time.

  • 1 metre = 100 centimetres
  • 1 kilometre = 1,000 metres
  • 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams
  • 1 litre = 1,000 millilitres
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes
Worked Example
Convert 3 metres to centimetres.
3 × 100 = 300 cm.

8. Geometry

Geometry is the study of shapes, lines, angles and space.

  • Triangle – 3 sides
  • Square – 4 equal sides
  • Rectangle – opposite sides equal
  • Pentagon – 5 sides
  • Hexagon – 6 sides

Types of Angles

  • Acute angle – less than 90°
  • Right angle – exactly 90°
  • Obtuse angle – greater than 90° but less than 180°

9. Perimeter and Area

Perimeter is the distance around a shape. Area is the amount of surface covered by a shape.

Worked Example – Rectangle
Length = 8 cm
Width = 5 cm

Perimeter = 8 + 5 + 8 + 5 = 26 cm
Area = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²

10. Data Handling

Data handling involves collecting, organizing, displaying and interpreting information.

Data can be shown using:

  • Tally charts
  • Tables
  • Bar graphs
  • Pictographs
Study Tip: Always read the title, labels and scale of a graph before answering questions.
