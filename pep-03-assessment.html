PEP Assessment Structure

Assessment breakdown – 2026

PEP assessments are conducted across Grades 4, 5 and 6. Each grade has specific assessment components that contribute to the student's overall profile.

Grade 4 – Literacy and Numeracy

Literacy Test – 30 multiple-choice questions.

Numeracy Test – 30 multiple-choice questions.

Grade 5 – Performance Tasks

Mathematics – up to 6 open-ended questions.

Language Arts – up to 6 open-ended questions.

Grade 6 – Final Assessments

Mathematics – 60 multiple-choice questions.

Language Arts – 60 multiple-choice questions.

Ability Test – 40 multiple-choice questions.

What does PEP assess?

AssessmentWhat it measures
LiteracyReading and language skills
NumeracyMathematical understanding
MathematicsCurriculum knowledge and problem-solving
Language ArtsGrammar, comprehension and writing
Ability TestVerbal and quantitative reasoning
Performance TasksApplying knowledge to practical situations
Important for parents: Students' assessment results from the different grade levels contribute to their PEP profile and secondary school placement.
