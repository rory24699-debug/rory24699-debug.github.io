PEP Preparation Guidance

Supporting Parents and Students in Grades 4–6

Welcome, Parents!

This section will help you support your child’s preparation for Jamaica’s Primary Exit Profile (PEP).

1. Understand PEP

Learn about the assessment, the subjects covered, and the importance of steady preparation.

2. Build a Study Routine

Set aside regular study time, provide a quiet space, and allow breaks between activities.

3. Practise Regularly

Use appropriate practice questions and learning resources. Review mistakes together and encourage your child.

4. Support Your Child

Encourage effort, celebrate progress, and make sure your child gets enough rest.
