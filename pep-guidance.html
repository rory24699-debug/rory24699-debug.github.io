PEP Preparation Guidance

Supporting Parents and Students in Grades 4–6

Welcome, Parents!

This section will help you support your child’s preparation for Jamaica’s Primary Exit Profile (PEP).

1. Understand PEP

Learn about the assessment, the subjects covered, and the importance of steady preparation.

2. Build a Study Routine

Set aside regular study time, provide a quiet space, and allow breaks between activities.

3. Practise Regularly

Use appropriate practice questions and learning resources. Review mistakes together and encourage your child.

4. Support Your Child

Encourage effort, celebrate progress, and make sure your child gets enough rest.

5. Understanding the PEP Assessment

PEP assesses students as they progress through Grades 4, 5 and 6. Parents should follow official Ministry of Education information for assessment details and updates.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is PEP?

PEP is Jamaica's Primary Exit Profile assessment programme.

2. Which grades participate?

Students in Grades 4, 5 and 6.

3. What subjects are assessed?

Mathematics, Language Arts and Ability.

4. How can parents help?

Encourage regular practice, reading and a consistent study routine.

5. Where can parents find official PEP information?

Visit the Ministry of Education's official PEP website.

6. Does PEPLink JA replace school or teachers?

No. It provides additional learning resources to support students.
