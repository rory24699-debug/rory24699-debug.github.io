📚 WHAT IS PEP? Nov 18th, 2026

Q: What is PEP?

Mess: PEP is Primary Exit Profile. It replaces GSAT. It checks what your child learned from Grade 4 to Grade 6.

📚 WHAT IS PEP? Nov 18th, 2026

Q: What grades do PEP?

Mess: Grade 4, Grade 5, and Grade 6.

📚 WHAT IS PEP? Nov 18th, 2026

Q: What subjects are in PEP?

Mess: Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: How is my child placed in high school?

Mess: By their total PEP score + the high school choices you list + space in the school.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: How many high schools can I choose?

Mess: You list 5 choices in order.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: What if my child does not get first choice?

Mess: They go to the next choice that has space and matches their score.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: Do I need to pay for PEP?

Mess: No, PEP in public schools is free.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: Can my child change high school choices?

Mess: Yes, before the deadline. Ask the school.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: What is the best high school?

Mess: The best is the one that fits your child's score, distance, and needs.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: Who do I ask if I am confused?

Mess: First, your child's teacher. Then the principal.

🏫 HIGH SCHOOL Nov 18th, 2026

Q: Where are the Ministry Regional Offices?

Mess: Contact your school first. If you need the Region, ask your school for the Regional Office number for your parish. They help with placement, transfers, and PEP questions.
