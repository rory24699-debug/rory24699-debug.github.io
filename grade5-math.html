Batch 1 - Numbers to 100,000

MOE Grade 5: Place value, rounding, multiples, factors, prime numbers.

  • What is the value of 7 in 47,325?
  • Round 68,472 to the nearest 1,000
  • List the factors of 24

Batch 2 - Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

MOE Grade 5: Equivalent fractions, add/subtract fractions, decimals to 2 places, % of a quantity.

  • 1/2 + 1/4 = ?
  • Write 0.75 as a fraction in simplest form
  • Find 50% of 120

Batch 3 - Multiplication & Division

MOE Grade 5: 2-digit x 2-digit, division with remainders, multi-step word problems.

  • 24 x 13 = ?
  • 145 ÷ 5 = ?
  • 5 boxes with 36 mangoes each. How many in all?

Batch 4 - Measurement

MOE Grade 5: Length, mass, capacity, time, money, perimeter, area, volume.

  • Perimeter of rectangle 8cm by 5cm = ?
  • Area of rectangle 8cm by 5cm = ?
  • Convert 2.5 m to cm

Batch 5 - Geometry & Statistics

MOE Grade 5: Angles, shapes, symmetry, coordinates, bar graphs, mode/median.

  • Name an angle that is 90°
  • How many sides does a hexagon have?
  • Find the mode: 3, 5, 5, 7, 5
