What is PEP?

PEP is Jamaica's national assessment program for primary school students. It assesses students' academic knowledge, skills and abilities over Grades 4, 5, and 6.

PEP helps to

  • Measure students' academic progress.
  • Identify students' strengths and areas needing improvement.
  • Assess readiness for secondary education.
  • Support students' transition from primary to secondary school.

Who participates

Students preparing to transition from primary school to Grade 7.

Remember: PEP is more than an examination. It provides a broader picture of a student's learning and development.
