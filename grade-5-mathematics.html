GRADE 5 - MATHEMATICS

MOE National Standards Curriculum - Grade 5 | PEP Performance Task Prep

✏️ PRACTICE - Grade 5 Maths 📝 PERFORMANCE TASKS - Maths

1. Number & Place Value - MOE Grade 5

What MOE expects: Read, write and compare numbers up to 100,000. Place value, rounding, odd/even, multiples and factors.

  • Place value to 100,000
  • Rounding to nearest 10, 100, 1000
  • Multiples, factors, prime numbers

2. Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

What MOE expects: Equivalent fractions, add/subtract fractions, decimals to 2 decimal places, link fractions-decimals-percentages.

  • Equivalent fractions & simplest form
  • Add / subtract fractions with same & different denominators
  • Decimals: add, subtract, compare
  • Percentages: 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% of a quantity

3. Computation - Whole Numbers

What MOE expects: Multi-digit multiplication and division, word problems, estimation.

  • 2-digit x 2-digit multiplication
  • Division with remainders
  • Order of operations / BODMAS intro
  • Multi-step word problems

4. Measurement

What MOE expects: Length, mass, capacity, time, money, perimeter, area and volume.

  • Convert cm/m/km, g/kg, ml/L
  • Perimeter of rectangles & composite shapes
  • Area of rectangles
  • Volume of cubes/cuboids
  • Time: 12 & 24 hour clock, elapsed time
  • Money: profit, loss, cost, change

5. Geometry

What MOE expects: Properties of 2D & 3D shapes, angles, symmetry, position.

  • Angles: acute, right, obtuse, reflex
  • Triangles & quadrilaterals - properties
  • Symmetry & reflection
  • Coordinates in first quadrant

6. Statistics & Patterns

What MOE expects: Collect, read and interpret data. Find patterns and rules.

  • Bar graphs, pictographs, line graphs
  • Mode, median, mean (intro)
  • Number patterns & growing patterns

Aligned to Jamaica MOE National Standards Curriculum (NSC) Grade 5 Mathematics. For PEP, Grade 5 Maths is assessed by Performance Task - open-ended problem solving & explaining your working.
