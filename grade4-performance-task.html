Real-Life Problems, Mathematical Reasoning & Applications

ACTIVITY 1: PLANNING A SHOPPING BUDGET

You are given $1,000 to purchase items for your home.

Rice – $250

Bread – $180

Milk – $220

Juice – $150

Task 1: Calculate the total cost.

Task 2: How much money will remain?

Task 3: If you add an item costing $120, will you stay within budget?

Suggested answers:

1. $250 + $180 + $220 + $150 = $800

2. $1,000 − $800 = $200

3. Yes. $920 total, leaving $80.
