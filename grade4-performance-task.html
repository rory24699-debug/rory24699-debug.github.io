MATHEMATICS PERFORMANCE TASK

Real-Life Problems, Mathematical Reasoning & Applications

ACTIVITY 1: PLANNING A SHOPPING BUDGET

Scenario: You are given $1,000 to purchase items for your home.

Rice – $250

Bread – $180

Milk – $220

Juice – $150

Task 1: Calculate the total cost.

Task 2: How much money will remain?

Task 3: If you add an item costing $120, will you stay within budget?

Suggested answers:

1. $250 + $180 + $220 + $150 = $800

2. $1,000 − $800 = $200

3. Yes. $920 total, leaving $80.

ACTIVITY 2: PLANNING A SCHOOL GARDEN

Scenario: Your class is helping to create a vegetable garden at school. The garden will be rectangular.

Length of garden: 8 metres

Width of garden: 5 metres

Number of rows for planting: 4

Seedlings in each row: 6

Task 1: What is the length of the garden?

Task 2: What is the width of the garden?

Task 3: Calculate the perimeter of the garden.

Task 4: How many seedlings will be planted altogether?

Task 5: If the class buys 30 seedlings, how many will be left over?

Suggested answers:

1. Length = 8 metres.

2. Width = 5 metres.

3. Perimeter = 8 + 5 + 8 + 5 = 26 metres.

4. 4 × 6 = 24 seedlings.

5. 30 − 24 = 6 seedlings left over.
