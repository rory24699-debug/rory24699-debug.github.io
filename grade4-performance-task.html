PRACTICE 1 | MONEY

1. Saving for a Book

Scenario: Jada saves $150 each week for 4 weeks. A book costs $550.

Task: How much does she save? Can she buy the book? How much left / short?

4 × $150 = $600
$600 - $550 = $50
Explanation: Multiply because she saves the same amount 4 times. $600 is more than $550, so yes she can buy it.

Answer: $600 saved. Yes. $50 left.

PRACTICE 2 | ADDITION / SUBTRACTION

2. Library Books

School library has 1,250 books. 340 are borrowed. 185 new books arrive.

Task: How many books are in the library now?

1,250 - 340 = 910
910 + 185 = 1,095
Explanation: First subtract borrowed books. Then add new books.

Answer: 1,095 books.

PRACTICE 3 | MULTIPLICATION

3. Juice Boxes for Sports Day

There are 8 teams. Each team has 9 players. Each player gets 2 juice boxes.

Task: How many juice boxes in all?

8 × 9 = 72 players
72 × 2 = 144 juice boxes
Explanation: First find total players, then multiply by 2 per player.

Answer: 144 juice boxes.

PRACTICE 4 | DIVISION

4. Sharing Oranges

96 oranges are shared equally among 8 children.

Task: How many does each child get? If 2 children give away 3 each, how many do they have left?

96 ÷ 8 = 12
12 - 3 = 9
Explanation: Divide equally. Then subtract what they gave away.

Answer: 12 each. 9 left after giving away.

PRACTICE 5 | FRACTIONS

5. Pizza Fractions

A pizza is cut into 8 equal slices. Kemar eats 3 slices. Maya eats 2 slices.

Task: What fraction did each eat? What fraction was eaten in all? What fraction is left?

Kemar: 3/8, Maya: 2/8
3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8 eaten
8/8 - 5/8 = 3/8 left
Explanation: Same denominator, so add numerators. Whole pizza = 8/8.

Answer: 5/8 eaten, 3/8 left.

PRACTICE 6 | TIME

6. School Trip Time

Bus leaves at 8:15 a.m. Trip takes 1 hour 45 minutes.

Task: What time does the bus arrive?

8:15 + 1 hour = 9:15
9:15 + 45 mins = 10:00 a.m.
Explanation: Add hours first, then minutes. 15 + 45 = 60 mins = 1 hour.

Answer: 10:00 a.m.

PRACTICE 7 | MEASUREMENT - LENGTH

7. Ribbon for Decorations

Each decoration needs 45 cm of ribbon. Class makes 6 decorations. Ribbon is sold in 3 m rolls. 1 m = 100 cm.

Task: How much ribbon needed? How many rolls?

6 × 45 cm = 270 cm
270 cm = 2 m 70 cm
1 roll = 300 cm, so 1 roll is enough
Explanation: Multiply for total. Convert cm to m. 270 is less than 300, so 1 roll covers it.

Answer: 270 cm needed. 1 roll.

PRACTICE 8 | MASS

8. Weighing Yams

Bag A: 2 kg 400 g. Bag B: 1 kg 850 g.

Task: Total mass? Which is heavier and by how much?

2,400 g + 1,850 g = 4,250 g = 4 kg 250 g
2,400 - 1,850 = 550 g
Explanation: Change kg to g to add/subtract easily. 1 kg = 1,000 g.

Answer: 4 kg 250 g total. Bag A heavier by 550 g.

PRACTICE 9 | CAPACITY

9. Filling Bottles

A tank holds 12 litres. Each bottle holds 500 mL. 1 L = 1,000 mL.

Task: How many bottles can be filled?

12 L = 12,000 mL
12,000 ÷ 500 = 24
Explanation: Convert to same unit (mL), then divide.

Answer: 24 bottles.

PRACTICE 10 | PERIMETER / AREA

10. Garden Fence

Rectangular garden: Length 10 m, Width 4 m.

Task: Find perimeter and area.

Perimeter = 10+4+10+4 = 28 m
Area = 10 × 4 = 40 m²
Explanation: Perimeter = distance around. Area = length × width. Units matter: m vs m².

Answer: Perimeter 28 m, Area 40 m².

PRACTICE 12 | MONEY / CHANGE

12. Market Shopping

Buy: Plantain $250, Callaloo $180, Tomatoes $320. Pay with $1,000.

Task: Total cost? Change?

250 + 180 + 320 = $750
1,000 - 750 = $250
Explanation: Add all items, then subtract from money given to get change.

Answer: $750 cost, $250 change.

PRACTICE 13 | PATTERNS

13. Number Pattern

Pattern: 7, 14, 21, 28, ...

Task: Rule? Next 3 numbers? What is the 10th term?

Rule: +7 each time
Next: 35, 42, 49
10th = 10 × 7 = 70
Explanation: This is the 7 times table. Each term = position × 7.

Answer: +7. Next 35, 42, 49. 10th = 70.

14. Shapes

A shape has 4 equal sides and 4 right angles.

Task: What shape is it? How many sides and angles?

4 equal sides + 4 right angles = Square
Explanation: A square has 4 equal sides and 4 right angles.

Answer: Square - 4 sides, 4 right angles.
