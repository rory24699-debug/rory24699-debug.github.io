ACTIVITY 1: PLANNING A SHOPPING BUDGET

Scenario: You are given $1,000 to purchase items for your home.

Study the shopping list below:

Rice – $250

Bread – $180

Milk – $220

Juice – $150

Task 1: Calculate the total cost of all four items.

Task 2: How much money will remain from $1,000?

Task 3: If you add an item costing $120, will you stay within your budget?

Suggested answers:

1. $250 + $180 + $220 + $150 = $800

2. $1,000 − $800 = $200

3. Yes. $800 + $120 = $920. You will have $80 remaining.
