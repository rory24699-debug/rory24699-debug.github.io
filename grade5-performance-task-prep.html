Mathematics Performance Tasks

Task 1: Planning a School Garden

A school plans to build a rectangular vegetable garden measuring 8 metres long and 5 metres wide. The school has a budget of $12,000.

  1. Calculate the perimeter of the garden.
  2. Calculate the area of the garden.
  3. Fencing costs $450 per metre. Calculate the total fencing cost.
  4. The school spends $3,500 on seeds and $2,800 on soil. How much money remains?
  5. Suggest one way to reduce the project's cost.
ANSWERS - Task 1:
1. Perimeter = 2 x (8+5) = 2 x 13 = 26m
2. Area = 8 x 5 = 40 sq m
3. Fencing = 26 x $450 = $11,700
4. Spent = $3,500 + $2,800 = $6,300. Remains = $12,000 - $6,300 = $5,700
5. Example: Use recycled wood for fencing / grow seeds from old plants / ask parents to volunteer.

Task 2: Organising a School Fair

A Grade 5 class plans to sell refreshments at a school fair.

ItemQuantityPrice
Juice40$150
Sandwiches30$250

  1. Calculate the money collected from selling all the juice.
  2. Calculate the expected income from all three items.
  3. If 20 sandwiches are sold, how many remain?
  4. The class spends $4,000 on supplies. Calculate the expected profit.
  5. Suggest two ways to keep accurate sales records.
ANSWERS - Task 2:
1. Juice = 40 x $150 = $6,000
2. Juice $6,000 + Sandwiches 30 x $250 = $7,500 + Cakes 25 x $120 = $3,000. Total = $16,500
3. Sandwiches remain = 30 - 20 = 10
4. Profit = $16,500 - $4,000 = $12,500
5. Example: Use a table / notebook, one person collects money, one person writes it down, count money at end of day.

Task 3: Planning a Class Trip

A Grade 5 class is planning a trip to a nature park.

  • 36 students and 4 teachers are attending.
  • Bus transportation costs $18,000.
  • Admission costs $300 per student.
  • Teachers enter free.
  • The class has collected $30,000.
  1. How many people will attend?
  2. Calculate the total student admission cost.
  3. Calculate the total cost of the trip.
  4. Is the money collected enough?
  5. Calculate how much additional money is needed, if any.
ANSWERS - Task 3:
1. People = 36 + 4 = 40 people
2. Admission = 36 x $300 = $10,800
3. Total cost = $18,000 + $10,800 = $28,800
4. Yes, enough - $30,000 is more than $28,800
5. Additional needed = $0. Money left = $30,000 - $28,800 = $1,200 left.

Task 4: Healthy Eating at School

Source A: School Notice

Green Valley Primary School is encouraging students to make healthier food choices. The school plans to introduce Healthy Eating Week. Students are encouraged to eat fruits, vegetables and balanced meals and reduce sugary drinks.

Source B: Student Survey

SnackStudents
Fruits12
Biscuits8
Sweets6
Chips4
  1. What is the main purpose of the school notice?
  2. Which snack was most popular?
  3. How many students chose biscuits or sweets?
  4. Give two benefits of eating fruits.
  5. Write a paragraph explaining why healthy eating is important.
ANSWERS - Task 4:
1. To encourage healthier food choices / Healthy Eating Week.
2. Fruits - 12 students - most popular.
3. Biscuits 8 + Sweets 6 = 14 students
4. Example: Fruits give vitamins, give energy, help you not get sick, help you concentrate.
5. Example paragraph: Healthy eating is important because it gives our bodies energy to learn and play. Fruits and vegetables keep us strong and help us not get sick. If we eat less sugar, our teeth stay healthy.

Task 5: Keeping Jamaica Clean

The residents of a Jamaican community have organised a clean-up campaign. They encourage residents to dispose of garbage properly, protect drains and avoid throwing plastic waste into rivers.

Community Survey

Waste ProblemReports
Plastic bottles15
Food containers10
Plastic bags12
Other garbage8
  1. What is the main objective of the campaign?
  2. Which waste problem received the most reports?
  3. Calculate the total number of reports.
  4. Suggest two ways residents can reduce garbage.
  5. Write a short essay explaining why keeping Jamaica clean is important.
ANSWERS - Task 5:
1. To encourage proper disposal, protect drains, stop plastic in rivers.
2. Plastic bottles - 15 reports
3. Total = 15+10+12+8 = 45 reports
4. Example: Recycle bottles, use reusable bags, compost food waste, don't litter.
5. Example: Keeping Jamaica clean is important because clean communities are healthy. When we throw garbage in drains, it causes flooding. When we keep beaches clean, tourists visit and Jamaica looks beautiful. We must all do our part.
