For Parents: What is PEP?

What is PEP?

PEP is the Primary Exit Profile. It is the exam all Grade 6 students in Jamaica do to move from primary school to high school. It replaced GSAT. It checks not just what your child remembers, but what they can understand and do.

How Does The Process Work?

  • Grade 4: Performance Tasks in Maths, Language Arts, Science & Social Studies
  • Grade 5: Performance Tasks + Ability Test - building critical thinking
  • Grade 6: Curriculum-Based Test + Performance Tasks + Ability Test - final score used for high school placement
