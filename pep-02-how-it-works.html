How PEP Works

PEP is completed over three primary school years: Grade 4, Grade 5 and Grade 6.

Students are assessed progressively as they move through primary school.

The process:

  • Grade 4 – Foundation: Students begin building the knowledge and skills needed for PEP.
  • Grade 5 – Development: Students continue learning and strengthening their academic abilities.
  • Grade 6 – Final Assessment: Students complete the required PEP assessments before transitioning to secondary school.

What happens at each stage?

Grade 4
Literacy and Numeracy Assessments
Students demonstrate reading, writing and mathematical skills.

Grade 5
Performance Tasks
Students apply their learning through practical assessment activities.

Grade 6
Final PEP Assessments
Students complete the Ability Test and Curriculum-Based Tests in Mathematics and Language Arts.

How is the final placement determined?

The Ministry uses students' assessment results to help determine their secondary school placement, taking parental school preferences into account where possible.
