Study Tips for Parents

Supporting Grades 4–6

1. Create a Study Routine

Choose a regular study time that fits your child's daily schedule.

2. Provide a Quiet Space

Find a comfortable place with good lighting and fewer distractions.

3. Encourage Short Study Sessions

Use manageable study periods with short breaks to help your child stay focused.

4. Practise and Review

Encourage your child to practise questions and review mistakes without fear.

5. Encourage and Support

Praise effort, allow enough sleep, and remind your child that progress takes time.
