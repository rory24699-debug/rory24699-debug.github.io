Secondary Schools Directory

The Secondary Schools Directory helps parents and guardians identify secondary schools across Jamaica when making their PEP school selections.

The directory provides information about schools according to their education region and parish.

What Information Can Parents Find?

  • Secondary school names.
  • School location and parish.
  • Education region.
  • Boys-only, girls-only and co-educational schools.
  • Technical and traditional high schools.
  • Schools available for PEP placement.

Official Ministry Receiving Schools List

The Ministry publishes a receiving-school list for parents to consult during the PEP school selection process.
