📘 Grade 4 Maths Notes

Read first, then watch, then practise

1. Numbers to 10,000

Place Value: 5,372 = 5,000 + 300 + 70 + 2
Thousands - Hundreds - Tens - Ones.
Example: In 7,481, the 7 is 7,000. The 4 is 400.

2. Addition & Subtraction

Line up the digits. Start from Ones. Regroup if needed.
Example: 2,456 + 1,329 = 3,785
Always check by estimating: 2,500 + 1,300 = 3,800 — close!

3. Multiplication

3 x 4 = 12. 3 groups of 4.
PEP Trick: 24 x 3 = (20 x 3) + (4 x 3) = 60 + 12 = 72

4. Division

12 ÷ 3 = 4. How many 3s in 12?
Check: 4 x 3 = 12, so you are correct.

5. Fractions

1/2 is half. 1/4 is quarter. Top = numerator, Bottom = denominator.
Example: 1/2 of 10 = 5. Half of 10 is 5.

6. Geometry

Square = 4 equal sides. Rectangle = 2 long, 2 short. Triangle = 3 sides. Circle = round, no sides.

7. Measurement & Money

1 metre = 100 cm. 1 kg = 1,000 g. 1 dollar = 100 cents.
Example: $5.50 + $2.25 = $7.75

8. Data & Graphs

Bar graph: taller bar = more. Always read the title and the scale first.
