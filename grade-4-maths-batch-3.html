Grade 4 Mathematics – Mock Test 3

Topic: Banana

Choose the correct answer for each question.

1. A farmer cuts 5 bunches of bananas with 12 bananas in each bunch. How many bananas altogether?

3. One bunch of bananas weighs 4 kilograms. What is the total weight of 6 bunches?
